# Business rules

Every rule below is enforced in the database (`supabase/schema.sql`), through row-level security and the functions the app calls. The client never decides on its own whether an action is allowed.

- A vendor can only read and change their own shop, products and orders.
- Customers can't read any table. They only see a shop's order page and their own order (through a private link).
- Stock is held the moment an order is placed, so two customers can't buy the last piece.
- Products: one price, or one list of choices (Size, Colour, Pack, whatever the shop calls it), each with its own price, optional sale price and stock. A sale price must be lower than the price; customers pay it and see the old price crossed out. Shops change a product's name, photo, prices, choices and stock any time; orders already placed keep the prices and names they were placed with, and a removed choice stays on old orders. All changes go through `save_product`, which checks them; vendors can only hide or show a product directly.
- An order is marked paid only by the vendor, never by the customer.
- Vendors can't change their own shop's status. Only the admin can suspend or reactivate a shop, with a reason.
- Only the admin can suspend a shop, close it for good, or delete it. Deleting needs the shop's link name typed and a reason, and the audit log keeps a note.
- One email, one role: an email that runs a shop can't be used to shop, and an email that has shopped can't open a shop.
- Customers must sign in to order. Only the customer who placed an order can say "I've sent the money" or "I've received it".
- A paid delivery order can only go out with the rider's name, phone number and a promised delivery time.
- Every order has a code made from the shop's initials (Bimpe's Beauty → BB-1043). Customers are asked to put it in their transfer note, and the vendor can search for it. The initials are set once and never change.
- Packing checklist: every item must be ticked as packed before an order goes to a rider or is collected. A packing photo is optional; it's the shop's proof if a customer says something was missing.
- Confirm or complain: up to 3 days after delivery (or once the promised time has passed), the customer who placed the order can report one problem (didn't arrive, wrong item, damaged, something missing, other) with up to 3 photos. The shop has 48 hours to refund, send it again, or explain. The customer then says it's sorted or asks ShopDesk to decide; if the shop doesn't answer in 48 hours, it is escalated to the admin automatically. The admin decides in Admin → Problems, and both sides see the decision.
- Order photos (packing and problem photos) are private: only the shop, the customer who placed the order, and the admin can see them.
- When the admin decides a customer was right, a refund can be set. The shop has 48 hours to pay it; after that its order page stops taking orders until the customer confirms the refund arrived (or the admin clears it). A shop with 3 or more complaints decided against it in 30 days shows in Needs attention.
- Chat: inside each order, between the shop and the customer who placed it, from the order until 7 days after it's done (longer while a problem or refund is open). Messages can't be edited or deleted. A customer sees a warning on any message with a different account number from the one on their order, and the admin is told (Needs attention). Either side can report the other's message (rude, threats, scam, spam). The admin can read a chat freely when the order has an escalated problem, a report or a flagged message; any other chat needs a reason. Every look is logged.
- Fair use, customers: a photo is needed for a wrong, damaged or missing item; at most 2 open problems and 3 reports a month; 2 complaints decided against them in 90 days pause reporting for 30 days. At most 3 unpaid orders per shop; unpaid orders cancel themselves after 48 hours (stock goes back) and the customer can cancel an unpaid order themselves. Reporting a shop needs an account: one open report per shop, 5 a day. The admin can block an abusive customer (they can't order, write or report) and unblock them, with a reason.
- Fair use, shops: every cancellation has a reason the customer sees; a shop can't cancel for a day after the customer says they've paid. A refund ordered by the admin needs the transfer receipt; while the customer checks, the shop's page stays open; no answer in 48 hours counts as received (the customer can still say it didn't arrive for 7 days). If the customer says it didn't arrive, the page pauses until they confirm or the admin clears it, and a new receipt doesn't end that.
- Records (Admin → Records): find any order by its code, with its problem, refund receipt, reports and chat. Deleting a shop (by the admin, or its account being removed in Supabase) first saves a private copy of everything it had, and its order photos stay; the copy can be downloaded with a reason. Automatic actions (unpaid cancels, refunds counted as received) show in the Log.
- Alerts: everyone gets alerts only about other people's actions (new order, money in, on its way, a message, a problem...). Signing out stops that phone's alerts.
- The vendor types a delivery fee only when their shop is set to "Customer pays delivery". Free-delivery shops never see a fee box.
- The cart and checkout: the shop page holds the products and the cart only; name, phone, delivery or pick-up and the address are on a separate checkout screen (a returning customer sees their saved details in one line, with Change), which is also where customers sign in. The cart: one per shop (each shop is paid by its own transfer), kept in the customer's browser for 30 days, so it survives a refresh, signing in or coming back later. When they come back, anything sold out leaves the cart and quantities come down to what's left, with a note saying so. Prices are always today's; the database checks stock and prices again when the order is placed.
- Customers type their delivery address at checkout. (Older orders placed with a shared location still show an **Open map pin** link for the rider; the pin goes with the address after 180 days.)
- ShopDesk is free for shops: no monthly plan, and a shop never closes for not paying. ShopDesk earns from its small fee on online payments and from ShopDesk Delivery.
- Nudges: a shop gets a phone alert (and the email backup) when orders have waited over 3 hours, when it has nothing for sale a day after approval, or when it has been away 5 days. Each kind is spaced out and capped, and only sent in the day.
- Pay online and ShopDesk Delivery (both off until switched on in Admin): a customer paying online pays the goods to the shop's own bank account through Paystack, plus the card fee and ShopDesk's fee on top, so the shop gets the full amount. While ShopDesk Delivery is on, every new delivery order goes with a courier the shop picks and is paid online only: the shop can't type its own fee, mark the money in or hand it to its own rider. ShopDesk pays the courier. Pick-up orders can still be paid by transfer.
- Payment is always in full, in one go: there are no part payments.
- The ShopDesk chat: each shop has one chat with ShopDesk inside its app (Shop tab, or the banner when ShopDesk has written). Everything ShopDesk sends shows as "ShopDesk", never who wrote it. Approving, not approving, suspending, closing and reopening a shop tell it there; the admin's reason for a suspension or closure stays in the audit log. Photos in it are private to the shop and the admin. Messages can't be edited or deleted.
- Coming soon: while Pay online or ShopDesk Delivery is off, the Shop tab shows it as Coming soon with an "I want this" button. Admin sees how many shops tapped it, and those shops get an alert when it's switched on.
- Visitors: a shop's page counts each visitor once a day (a random code on their phone, or their account), not the shop's own visits. The shop hears "Someone is looking at your shop" at most every 2 hours, from 7am to 10pm, and sees today's and this week's visitors on the Sales tab. Visits are kept 90 days.
- ShopDesk Delivery offers door-to-door couriers only: the courier collects from the shop and delivers to the customer's door.
- Live tracking: for an order with the shop's own rider, the shop can send the rider a private link. While the rider's page is open and sharing, the customer and the shop see the rider's latest position on a map. Only the latest position is kept, only while the order is on its way, and old positions are removed after a day.
- Data retention: `select purge_customer_details(180);` blanks customer details (name, phone, address, map pin) on orders closed more than 180 days ago, including in deleted shops' copies. Run it monthly from the SQL Editor.
